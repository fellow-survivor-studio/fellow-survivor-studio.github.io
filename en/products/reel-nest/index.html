Frequently asked questions

Contact the team ↗
What media can I save?

You can save downloadable media detected by the app on supported web pages. Only use it for content you own or have the rights or permission to access, download and use. Reel Nest does not bypass DRM, encryption, authentication, paywalls or other technical access controls.

How do Free and Pro differ?

With Free, watching an ad unlocks downloads for 5 minutes, and you can store up to 500 MB in Nest. A monthly or annual Pro subscription removes the ad requirement for downloads and the in-app storage limit. Actual storage depends on free space on your device.

Is my media backed up to the cloud?

Reel Nest does not provide cloud backup, sync or data recovery. Media and app data are stored on your device and may be lost when you uninstall the app or change devices. Please manage any backups you need.

Which devices are supported?

Reel Nest supports Android 7.0 and later, as listed on Google Play. Check Google Play for availability on your device and the latest requirements.
