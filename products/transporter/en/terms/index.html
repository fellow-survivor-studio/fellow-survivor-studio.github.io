About this policy

This policy sets out the rules for using the Android app Transporter ("the app") and what Fellow Survivor Studio ("we") does when they are broken. It applies to everyone who installs and uses the app and takes effect on November 7, 2026.

The app forwards messages received on your phone to numbers you choose. Messages carry information about the people who sent and receive them, so the heart of this policy is using the app without violating anyone else's privacy or the confidentiality of their communications. How we handle personal information is covered separately in the Transporter Privacy Policy.

1. Permitted use

You may use the app only to forward messages received on your own phone to your own numbers or to people who have agreed to receive them.

  • Receiving your messages on another phone or number you use (roaming abroad, carrying two phones, etc.)
  • Forwarding messages on a work phone to the person responsible, when the phone's owner (the company) and that person have agreed
  • Forwarding messages on a family member's phone to the family member who looks after them, when the person using the phone knows and has agreed

If the owner of a destination number does not want to receive messages, you must stop forwarding to that number right away.

2. Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited. Many of them are also crimes under laws on wiretapping, privacy, spam and stalking.

  1. Installing the app on someone else's phone without their knowledge, or taking their messages without the consent of the person using the phone
  2. Using someone else's verification codes to get into their accounts or make financial transactions, or using the app for phishing or other fraud
  3. Sending advertising messages that recipients have not agreed to, or using the app as a bulk-messaging tool
  4. Harassing someone by repeatedly sending them messages they do not want
  5. Modifying or tampering with the app to get around ads or paid features, or clicking ads deliberately or automatically
  6. Distributing modified versions of the app or impersonating us
  7. Using the app for anything else that breaks the law

3. What we do about violations

The app has no user accounts and we never see your messages, so when we receive a report we act within the limits below.

SituationWhat we do
Someone reports their messages were taken or received without consentWe explain how to find and remove the app from their phone and where to report it, and consider features that prevent the same misuse
Invalid ad clicks, getting around paid featuresWe may cancel paid features or refuse refunds, and report it to Google
Distributing modified versions, impersonating usWe ask the distributor to remove them and may take legal action
Lawful requests from law enforcementWe cooperate, but we hold no message content or forwarding history

If you are receiving unwanted messages through this app, block the sending number and report it to your local spam or police authority (in Korea: the Illegal Spam Response Center, 118, spam.kisa.or.kr, or the police, 112). Please also tell us using the contact in section 7.

4. Changes to and discontinuation of the service

We may add, change or remove features, or stop offering the app. We will announce changes that are less favourable to you at least 7 days in advance, and discontinuation at least 30 days in advance, in the app.

  • The app forwards up to 10 messages a day for free, and you can remove the limit by watching an ad or subscribing (section 5).
  • Changes to Android, carriers or messaging apps may stop some features (especially receiving RCS chat messages) from working without notice. We will fix them or let you know as soon as we can.
  • If we stop offering the app, the installed app and the data on your phone remain until you delete them.

5. Free daily limit, ads and subscription

  • Free limit: you can forward up to 10 messages a day. One forward means one received message sent to one destination number; a long message split into several texts still counts as one. Failed sends, spam blocks and filtered verification-code messages do not count. The limit refills every day at midnight in your phone's time zone, and changing the phone's clock does not add more.
  • Messages over the limit: they are held instead of sent. They are sent in the order received once you watch an ad to the end, subscribe, or the limit refills the next day. Messages held for more than 24 hours after they arrived are not sent and stay only in the history.
  • Rewarded ads: if you choose to watch an ad and watch it to the end, you can forward without a limit until midnight. Closing the ad early does not remove the limit. Ads are provided by Google and are sometimes unavailable; if so, please try again later.
  • Subscription: subscribing through Google Play removes the daily limit for the subscription period, and you do not need to watch ads. The price, billing period and any free trial are shown on the subscription screen. Carrier charges for forwarded texts are not included.
  • Automatic renewal and cancellation: unless you cancel, a subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period and is charged to the same Google account. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • Price changes and free trials: before raising the price or before a free trial turns into a paid subscription, we will notify you in advance and ask for your consent where the law requires it. If you do not agree, you can cancel before the next billing date.
  • Refunds: if you ask by email or through Google Play within 7 days of a payment, we refund that payment in full, even if you have already used the subscription (the withdrawal period under Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce). After 7 days we do not refund the period you have paid for, and if you cancel you keep access until it ends. If the app did not work as described, we refund you as the law requires even after 7 days, and if the law where you live gives you a longer period, that period applies. A purchase made by a minor without a legal guardian's consent can be cancelled.
  • If we stop offering the app while your subscription period has time left, we will refund the remaining period.
  • We will announce changes to the free limit, ads or subscription that are less favourable to you in advance, as described in section 4.

6. Limitation of liability

Forwarding depends on your phone and carrier and may be delayed or missed, so do not rely on the app alone for messages you cannot afford to miss, such as banking or work messages.

  • Messages may not be forwarded if your phone is off, has no signal, has battery-saving that stops the app, or has permissions turned off.
  • You pay any carrier charges for forwarded messages. Long messages are split into several, and roaming may cost more.
  • You choose the filters and destination numbers, so you are responsible for messages sent to a number you entered by mistake.
  • You are responsible for any harm after you turn on forwarding of verification-code messages. The app warns you when you turn it on.
  • You are responsible for disputes that arise because you broke this policy.

However, we are responsible for harm caused by our intent or gross negligence, and for any liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

7. Contact and appeals

Send questions, reports and appeals against our actions to fellow.survivor.studio@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days; if it will take longer, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

When reporting, include the date, what happened and the app version (Settings > About). If you send message text or screenshots, please hide verification codes and other people's numbers.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the app and on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect, or at least 30 days before for changes that are less favourable to you. If you do not agree with the changes, you can stop using the app by uninstalling it.
