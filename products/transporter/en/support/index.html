Contact us

Write an email

Email: support@fellowsurvivor.com

We reply to questions within 7 days and to privacy requests (access, deletion and so on) within 10 days. If it will take longer, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer. There is no live chat.

What to include

  • The date and time it happened and what is not working
  • The app version (Settings > About), your phone model and Android version
  • The conditions of the filter involved (you may hide the destination numbers)

Do not send verification codes, passwords, or other people's phone numbers or messages. If you attach screenshots, please hide this information.

Cancelling a subscription and refunds

You can cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it. You can ask for a refund at the email address above or through Google Play; the rules are in section 5 of the Terms of Use and Acceptable Use Policy. Google handles payment details, so please do not send card numbers.

If you are receiving unwanted messages

If you are receiving unwanted messages through this app, block the sending number and report it to your local spam or police authority (in Korea: the Illegal Spam Response Center, 118, spam.kisa.or.kr, or the police, 112). Please also tell us at the email address above. How we handle reports is described in the Terms of Use and Acceptable Use Policy.
