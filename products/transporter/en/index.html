About Transporter

Transporter is an Android app that forwards text messages received on your phone to numbers you choose, based on conditions you set. Received messages and forwarding history are stored only on your phone, and we run no server that receives your messages.

There is no sign-up, so there is no account to create or delete. You can clear the history on your phone in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

This website

This website is served through Cloudflare, and the former address (fellow-survivor-studio.github.io) is forwarded here by GitHub Pages. The pages contain no ads, visitor analytics, forms that send what you type, or tracking cookies. Cloudflare, which delivers the site, and GitHub, which forwards the former address, may process your IP address and other information needed to provide and secure their services under their own policies. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email links open your own mail app. Nothing is sent to us until you send the email yourself.
